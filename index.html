Welcome to CodeHub

CodeHub is a beginner-friendly guide to computer programming. Whether you are writing your very first line of code or exploring how computers execute instructions under the hood, this site breaks down core terminology, syntax symbols, and hands-on coding lessons.

What is Coding?

Coding (or programming) is the process of writing step-by-step instructions that a computer can understand and execute. Different programming languages are designed for different tasks—some prioritize human readability, while others give you direct control over hardware and memory.
